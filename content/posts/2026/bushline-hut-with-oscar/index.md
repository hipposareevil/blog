Oscar and I went up to [Bushline Hut](https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/nelson-tasman/places/nelson-lakes-national-park/things-to-do/huts/bushline-hut/) with Ethan and Craig. We didn't have it to ourselves but still had plent of room.

It was uber hot on the hike in, but once we got to the hut the wind picked up and it was chilly.  We carried extra wood and pine cones up so we could get a proper fire going. 

It got super windy at night and even broke the tent of a gal staying across the way. We got a dusting of snow in the morning and it was still windy.

We headed over Mt. Robert to the warming hut before going down to the cars. The kids got blown over a few times but had a good time anyway.
